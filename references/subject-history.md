# History

Use: chronology -> context -> event or institution -> causes -> process -> consequences -> perspectives -> evidence-based evaluation.

## Layout

Use timelines, maps, source excerpts, cause-effect chains, and comparison tables. Keep chronology visible when discussing multiple actors or stages.

## Required Checks

- Verify dates, names, places, and sequence.
- Separate primary evidence, later interpretation, and teaching summary.
- Avoid presenting a contested interpretation as undisputed fact.
- Explain short-term and long-term causes and effects separately.
- Keep visual reconstructions labeled as illustrations rather than documentary evidence.
