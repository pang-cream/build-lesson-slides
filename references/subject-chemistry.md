# Chemistry

Use the macro-micro-symbolic sequence: observable phenomenon -> particle explanation -> chemical representation -> conditions -> evidence -> application or calculation.

## Layout

Use three-way comparison pages for macroscopic observation, microscopic model, and equation. Keep reaction conditions adjacent to the reaction arrow. Use precise diagrams for apparatus.

## Required Checks

- Balance every equation and verify charge, state symbols, and conditions.
- Distinguish observation from explanation.
- Check units, significant figures, and limiting assumptions.
- State laboratory hazards, protective measures, and disposal when relevant.
- Do not use generated illustrations as evidence of an exact apparatus setup.
